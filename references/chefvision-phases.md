# ChefVision phases — adapted to Anime

Source: ChefVision AI Cooking ASMR master prompt, sent by the owner on 31/10/2026. The original is hyper-realistic, top-down, 9:16, 8-12 chronological scenes, everything in one pan or bowl where possible, physical realism, no floating or morphing.

## Adaptations for this skill (mandatory)

1. Style: replace hyper-realistic with the Anime style lock in SKILL.md. Never mix hyper-realistic + Anime in one film.
2. Character: the original mostly shows hands and has no character sheet. This skill adds a character sheet before Phase 3.
3. Dialogue: keep Vietnamese dialogue minimal for a pure ASMR film (1 opening line + 1 closing line). ASMR cooking sounds dominate.
4. Thumbnail: the original asks for 16:9. For Shorts, use the 9:16 poster from Step 4 instead.

## The 5 phases

- Phase 1: recipe, format and style lock. Confirm the dish, the pan, the character and the 8 x 10s format.
- Phase 2: scene breakdown in cooking order. Do not force steps the recipe does not have (for example, do not force a topping step on a cake that has none).
- Phase 3: image prompts, one JSON block per scene.
- Phase 4: video motion prompts, one JSON block per scene.
- Phase 5: poster + SEO, then render, assembly and QC.

## Phase gates

Stop at the end of every phase for approval. Never advance without approval. The original narrative structure, adapted:

- Scene 1: base or pan preparation.
- Scenes 2-4: layering and mixing.
- Scene 5: mixing or pouring into the pan.
- Scene 6: topping or putting into the oven, only if the recipe has it.
- Scene 7: oven or golden reveal.
- Scene 8 (and 9-12 if the recipe needs them): presentation, slicing, first bite.

## Locks

- Visual: warm morning studio lighting, rustic dark oak table, springform pan or cast-iron skillet where the recipe fits.
- Audio: real cooking sounds, clear per action, no music change mid-film.
- Camera: one camera move per shot.
