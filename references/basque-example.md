## Breakdown (Phase 2)

1. 0-10s: Line the pan. Bunny presses parchment paper into the pan, paper folds naturally. ASMR: paper rustling, light pressing.
2. 10-20s: Beat cream cheese + sugar until smooth and glossy. ASMR: whisking, creamy texture.
3. 20-30s: Pour eggs + fresh cream in a slow stream. ASMR: pouring liquid, small drops.
4. 30-40s: Sift flour, add vanilla, stir gently. ASMR: sifting, gentle stirring.
5. 40-50s: Pour batter into the pan, tap lightly to release bubbles, flat glossy surface. ASMR: thick pouring, pan tapping.
6. 50-60s: Put the pan into the oven, close the door. ASMR: oven door closing, oven running quietly.
7. 60-70s: Open the oven, deep golden-brown burnt top, puffed, gently jiggling. ASMR: oven door opening, heat, gentle jiggle.
8. 70-80s: Slice, soft molten centre, spoon lifts the first bite, bunny smiles. ASMR: soft slicing, spoon scooping, molten flow.
